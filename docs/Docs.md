# **Moovy: The Eliza Project**

**Moovy** is an intelligent conversational agent. Taking on the role of AI developers and digital consultants, we designed this chatbot to identify and solve a real business need: the "choice paralysis" often experienced by movie viewers.

---

##  **Key Functionalities**

Moovy features a text-based interface and a custom response generation mechanism designed for natural communication.

* **Personalized Recommendations:** Provides tailored suggestions based on your unique profile and taste preferences.
* **Genre-Specific Recommendations:** Allows users to request 3–5 recommendations for a precise genre (e.g., Animation, Romance) regardless of their usual habits.
* **"Lookalike" Engine:** Suggests films that match the specific style or vibe of a movie you already love, such as *Titanic*.
* **Discord Integration:** The bot is fully testable on the Discord messaging platform.
* **External Data Integration:** Moovy can consume structured data from open APIs to enhance the conversational experience.

---

## **Business & Marketing Strategy**

### **Market Analysis**
* **Target Audience:** Digital natives and frequent viewers using platforms like Netflix or Disney+.
* **The Problem:** While 85% of people watch films regularly, they often struggle to make a decision without a clear starting point.
* **Objectives:** Our goals include high reactivity, automation, and improved user engagement.

### **Monetization & ROI**
Moovy utilizes a **Freemium model** to illustrate the Return On Investment (ROI):
* **Free Users:** Standard access with daily limits.
* **Premium Subscribers:** Unlock advanced filters (search by cast, mood, or platform) and unlimited credits.

### **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**
We track the business impact through specific KPIs:
* **Efficiency:** Maintaining response latency under 5 seconds.
* **Growth:** Targeting 1,500 Monthly Active Users (MAU) at launch.
* **Retention:** Measuring loyalty and satisfaction through returning user rates.

##  **Ethical Considerations**
As AI developers, we conducted an ethical impact assessment to ensure responsible use of technology.

### **1. Prevention of the ELIZA Effect**
* **The Concept:** Users often project human traits and feelings onto rudimentary computer programs, a phenomenon known as the "ELIZA effect".
* **Our Action:** To avoid creating dependency or user psychosis, Moovy uses transparently algorithmic language to maintain its status as a tool, not a companion.

### **2. Privacy & Fairness**
* **Action:** We ensure user data is handled responsibly and transparently, respecting the "right to be forgotten".
* **Goal:** To prevent algorithmic biases and the spread of misinformation.

### **3. Combatting "Filter Bubbles"**
* **Action:** We utilize a "Serendipity" variable to ensure recommendations include independent cinema and "hidden gems," broadening the user's cultural horizon.
